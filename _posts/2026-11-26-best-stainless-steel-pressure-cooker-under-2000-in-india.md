## First, the law: every pressure cooker must carry the ISI mark

Pressure cookers are not an optional ISI product. Under the Domestic Pressure Cooker (Quality Control) Order, 2020 (S.O. 294(E), dated 21 January 2020), every domestic pressure cooker sold in India must conform to IS 2347:2017 and bear the BIS Standard Mark under a BIS licence. Only cookers made for export are exempt.

This has been enforced on Amazon itself. In August 2022 the Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA) found that 2,265 pressure cookers that did not meet the mandatory standard had been sold through Amazon after the order came in. It directed Amazon to notify those buyers, recall the cookers, refund them and pay a ₹1 lakh penalty. Flipkart got a similar order for 598 cookers.

### How to check the ISI mark yourself

A printed ISI logo is easy to copy, so check the licence number. Look for "CM/L-" followed by a number near the ISI mark on the cooker body or the box. Then:

1. Install the free BIS Care app from the Bureau of Indian Standards.
2. Open "Verify Licence Details" and enter the number.
3. Check that the licence holder and brand match your cooker, the standard is IS 2347 and the licence is in force.

If the number does not match the brand or the product, send it back. Of our six picks, Butterfly, Prestige and Greenchef say "ISI" in the listing itself. For the others, ask for or check the CM/L number on delivery.

## A 3 litre cooker does not cook 3 litres of food

The size on the box is the volume of the body, not how much food you can cook. Hawkins' own product information sets three fill limits that apply to any pressure cooker:

- **Two-thirds full** for solid foods such as vegetables or meat.
- **Half full** for rice, soups and other foods that expand or froth.
- **One-third full** for dals that froth and sprout out of the vent, especially tuvar and moong, counting dal and water together.

So in practice a 3 litre cooker handles about 2 litres of vegetables, about 1.5 litres of rice and water, and only about 1 litre of tuvar dal with water. Overfilling can block the vent and build up excess pressure. If you regularly cook dal for four or more, look at a 5 litre cooker instead, which is usually above this budget. ## How to choose a stainless steel pressure cooker

### Inner lid or outer lid

Inner lid cookers have a lid that goes in through the mouth of the cooker and seals from inside, the classic Hawkins design. Outer lid cookers close over the rim and turn to lock, like the Butterfly, Prestige, Pigeon and Greenchef here. Pick the style you are used to; both are covered by IS 2347.

### Base and induction

If you cook on induction, the base must be magnetic. All six listings say gas and induction. A triply or sandwich base spreads heat better and reduces scorching, which is why Butterfly, Prestige and Greenchef highlight it. Pair it with our [induction cooktop picks]({{ "/best-induction-cooktop-under-3000-in-india/" | relative_url }}) or [2 burner gas stove picks]({{ "/best-2-burner-gas-stove-under-3000-in-india/" | relative_url }}).

### Safety parts and spares

Look for at least two pressure release paths: the weight or whistle, plus a metallic safety plug or safety valve, and ideally a gasket release system. Then make sure gaskets and safety valves for your model are easy to buy, since they wear out and are not covered by the warranty.
