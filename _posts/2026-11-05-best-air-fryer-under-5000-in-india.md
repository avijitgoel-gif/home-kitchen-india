## How to choose an air fryer

### 1. Pick the right capacity

- **2 to 3.5 litres:** one or two people, snacks.
- **4 to 4.5 litres:** families of two to four. The best choice for most homes.
- **5 litres and up:** families of four or more, or batch cooking.

### 2. Wattage affects speed and your bill

Most 4 litre air fryers draw 1200W to 1500W. A typical 15-minute cook uses about 0.3 to 0.4 units of electricity, so running costs are small.

### 3. Digital or manual?

Digital models add presets and precise timers. Manual dials are simpler and have fewer parts to fail. Both cook the same way.

### 4. Check the basket coating

Look for a food-safe, PFAS-free or silicone-coated basket, and avoid metal utensils that scratch the coating.

### 5. Ignore the MRP

Several listings show MRPs two to three times the selling price, and some search results show impossible MRPs. Compare the selling price and rating count.
