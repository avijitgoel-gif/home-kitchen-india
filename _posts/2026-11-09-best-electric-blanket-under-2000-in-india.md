## Is the ISI mark compulsory for electric blankets?

It is now. Electric blankets, heating pads and similar flexible heating appliances fall under the government's Safety of Household, Commercial and Similar Electrical Appliances Quality Control Order, which requires them to meet IS 302 (Part 1): 2024 and carry the ISI mark under a BIS licence ([BIS circular on the 2025 order](https://www.services.bis.gov.in/tmp/Circular_ySsH_2025-05-20.pdf)). According to a compliance summary of the 2026 order (S.O. 1739(E), dated 6 April 2026), the rule applies to general enterprises from 1 October 2026, small enterprises from 1 January 2027 and micro enterprises from 1 April 2027 ([ERA Global summary](https://www.eraglobal.co.in/blankets-pads-clothing-and-similar-flexible-heating-appliances/)).

In practice that means many small brands may still be selling without the mark for a few more months. Look for the ISI mark and a CM/L licence number on the product images or label, not just the words "ISO certified" or "ZED certified".

## How to choose an electric blanket

### 1. Under blanket or over blanket

Every pick here is an under blanket. It goes flat on the mattress, under your bedsheet, and you sleep on top of it. Do not use an under blanket as a cover. Over blankets and heated throws exist, but most good ones cost more than ₹2,000.

### 2. Auto cut-off and overheat protection

This is the most important feature. A blanket with auto cut-off switches itself off when it gets too hot. If the listing does not mention it, assume it does not have it and switch the blanket off before you sleep.

### 3. Size

A single bed under blanket is usually about 150 x 75 cm. It warms your sleeping area, not the whole mattress. Couples should buy two singles or a double, and most double bed blankets cost more than ₹2,000.

### 4. Washability

None of the four picks here is machine washable. Use a bedsheet over it and spot clean the blanket only, following the manual.

### 5. Warranty terms

A "10 year" or "5 year" claim can mean different things. Tapish, for example, covers the controller for 5 years and the blanket for 1 year. Read the full warranty line before you buy.

## Electric blanket safety rules

- Lay it completely flat. Never fold, bunch or roll it while it is switched on.
- Do not put heavy objects on it, and do not use it on a folding bed or sofa where it can crease.
- If the listing does not say auto cut-off, switch it off before you sleep. Bell's listing, for example, says to switch off after a maximum of 60 minutes.
- Do not use it with a hot water bag, and keep it away from water.
- Do not use it for infants, or for anyone who cannot feel heat or switch it off themselves.
- Stop using it if you see scorch marks, a damaged cord or a controller that gets hot.
