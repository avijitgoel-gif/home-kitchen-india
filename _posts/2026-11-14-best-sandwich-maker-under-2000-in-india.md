## What "auto cut off" really means

Almost every listing here says "auto cut off". Prestige's own manual for the PGMFB explains how it works in practice. You plug it in and the red light comes on. The green ready light comes on when the plates reach a preset temperature, which usually takes a few minutes. When you close a sandwich inside, the green light goes off, and it glows again when toasting is over. To stop the machine, the manual tells you to unplug it.

In other words, a thermostat holds a set temperature. There is no timer, and the manual's way to stop it is to pull the plug. Treat auto cut off as overheating protection, not a reason to leave it plugged in. The manual also says never to leave it unattended, use it near curtains or put metal foil inside.

Two more tips from the manual protect the non-stick coating, which is the most common thing to fail:

1. **Do not force the lid shut on an overfilled sandwich.** Reduce the filling instead. Pressing hard damages the appliance and the coating.
2. **Never use a metal knife or fork to lift the sandwich out.** Use a plastic or wooden spatula.

## Grill plates or sandwich plates?

Grill plates (like the PGMFB) have ridges, press the sandwich flat and cope better with odd bread sizes and thick fillings. Sandwich plates cut and seal the bread into two triangles, which keeps fillings in for tiffin boxes but needs bread that fits the plate. All five picks above use grill style plates. If you want sealed triangles, look for "sandwich plate" or "toast" in the model name.

## The ISI rule from 1 October 2026

The Safety of Household, Commercial and Similar Electrical Appliances (Quality Control) Order, 2026 (S.O. 1739(E), 6 April 2026) came into force on 1 October 2026. It requires electrical appliances for household use rated up to 250V single phase to meet IS 302 (Part 1):2024 and carry the ISI mark under a BIS licence. Small enterprises get until 1 January 2027 and micro enterprises until 1 April 2027, and makers can sell declared older stock for up to six months. The BIS safety standard written specifically for toasters, grills and sandwich makers is IS 302 (Part 2/Sec 9).

What this means for you: look for the ISI mark and a CM/L licence number on the box or rating plate, and be wary of unbranded sandwich makers that show neither. Prestige says its PGMFB and PGMFD are ISI certified on their Amazon listings.
