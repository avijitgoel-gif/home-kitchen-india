## How to choose a room heater

### 1. Pick the right heater type

- **Fan heaters** (Orpat, AmazonBasics, Orient) heat the whole closed room fastest. They are a little noisy.
- **Quartz heaters** (Usha, Havells) are silent and warm whatever is in front of them. Good for small rooms and close-up use.
- **Radiant heaters** (Bajaj Flashy) are the simplest and cheapest, and also warm only what is in front of them.

### 2. Match wattage to your room

For a small bedroom or study, 800W to 1000W is usually enough if you sit close to the heater. To warm a whole closed room, a 2000W fan heater does the job faster. Models with two heat settings let you drop the power once the room is warm.

### 3. Work out the running cost

Electricity is billed in units, where 1 unit is 1 kWh. A 2000W heater running for 1 hour uses 2 units. An 800W heater running for 1 hour uses 0.8 units. If your tariff is ₹7 per unit, that is about ₹14 an hour for the 2000W heater and about ₹5.60 an hour for the 800W one. Check your own electricity bill for your exact tariff.

| Heater power | Units per hour | Cost per hour at ₹7/unit | Cost for 4 hours a day, 30 days |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 800W | 0.8 | ₹5.60 | ₹672 |
| 1000W | 1.0 | ₹7.00 | ₹840 |
| 2000W | 2.0 | ₹14.00 | ₹1,680 |

### 4. Look for the ISI mark

Room heaters are covered by Indian Standard IS 302 (Part 2/Sec 30) and are on the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) list of products under compulsory ISI certification ([BIS Scheme I list](https://www.bis.gov.in/product-certification/products-under-compulsory-certification/scheme-i-mark-scheme/?lang=en)). Check for the ISI mark on the product or in the listing images before you buy, especially from unknown brands.

### 5. Prefer safety features

Overheat protection, tip-over protection and a cool-touch body are worth having, especially with children or pets at home. Never cover a heater, never dry clothes on it, and keep it at least a metre from curtains and bedding.

## FAQ

### Which is the best room heater under ₹2,000 in India?
For most homes, the Orpat OEH-1220 2000W fan heater at about ₹1,099. It has 4.1 stars from about 57,500 Amazon ratings. If you want a quieter, cheaper-to-run option, the Usha 2 Rod 800W quartz heater at about ₹1,199 is a good pick.

### How much electricity does a 2000W room heater use?
A 2000W heater uses 2 units of electricity per hour at full power. At ₹7 per unit, that is about ₹14 per hour. Using the lower heat setting reduces this.

### Fan heater or quartz heater, which is better?
A fan heater warms a whole closed room faster. A quartz heater is silent and warms only what is in front of it, and the 800W models use less power. Choose a fan heater for a bedroom and a quartz heater for sitting close by.

### Is the ISI mark compulsory for room heaters?
Yes. Room heaters under IS 302 (Part 2/Sec 30) are on the BIS list of products under compulsory ISI certification.

### Is it safe to leave a room heater on overnight?
It is safer not to. If you must, use a model with overheat and tip-over protection, keep it away from bedding and curtains, and keep a door slightly open for ventilation.
