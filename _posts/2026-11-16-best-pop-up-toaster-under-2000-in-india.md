## Do more watts mean faster toast?

Not by much in this group. The range is only 700W to 850W, and all six are two slice toasters with a browning dial that sets the time. A higher setting simply runs longer. Features matter more day to day: reheat warms toast that has gone cold without browning it again, and defrost lets you toast bread straight from the fridge or freezer. Wide slots matter if you buy thick cut or bakery bread. Bajaj says "extra wide", Morphy Richards says "wide" and Crompton gives a number, 38mm.

## Is ISI compulsory for toasters?

Yes, from 1 October 2026. The Safety of Household, Commercial and Similar Electrical Appliances (Quality Control) Order, 2026 (S.O. 1739(E), dated 6 April 2026) requires electrical appliances for household use rated up to 250V single phase to meet IS 302 (Part 1):2024 and carry the ISI mark under a BIS licence. Small enterprises have until 1 January 2027 and micro enterprises until 1 April 2027, and declared older stock can be sold for up to six months. The BIS safety standard written for toasters, grills and similar appliances is IS 302 (Part 2/Sec 9). Look for the ISI mark and a CM/L licence number on the box or rating plate, especially on unbranded toasters.

## How to use a pop-up toaster safely

The UK charity Electrical Safety First and toaster makers give the same advice:

- **Never put a knife, fork or anything metal into the slots.** If bread gets stuck, switch off, unplug, let it cool and ease it out, or use wooden tongs.
- **Empty the crumb tray often.** Built-up crumbs can catch fire. Oster's manual says to empty it after each use.
- **Unplug before cleaning**, wipe the outside with a damp cloth only, and never put water or cleaner inside.
- **Use a lower setting for a single slice** or dry bread, and a higher one for fresh or whole wheat bread.
- **Stop using it** if you notice burning smells, sparks or smoke, or if it trips the breaker.

Several of the review summaries above mention burnt bread or fire, so these points are worth taking seriously.
