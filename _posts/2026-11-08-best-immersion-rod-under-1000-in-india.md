## How to choose an immersion rod

### 1. Pick the wattage

Most rods are 1500W, which suits a normal 15 to 20 litre bathing bucket. A 1000W rod is fine for a smaller bucket or if you can wait a few extra minutes.

### 2. Check the plug and socket

A 1500W heater draws about 6.5 amps at 230V. That is at the limit of a normal 6A socket, so a 16A power socket is the safer choice. Never run a rod through a cheap multi-plug extension.

### 3. Look for the ISI mark

Electric immersion water heaters (IS 368:2014) are on the Bureau of Indian Standards list of products under compulsory certification ([BIS Scheme I list](https://www.bis.gov.in/product-certification/products-under-compulsory-certification/scheme-i-mark-scheme/?lang=en)). Every rod in our list says ISI or ISI certified in its listing except the Usha and the Bajaj 1000W, whose bullets do not mention it. Check the product images for the ISI mark and CM/L licence number before you buy.

### 4. Prefer a bucket clip and water marks

A clip or hook keeps the element away from the bucket wall, so a plastic bucket does not melt. MIN and MAX marks tell you how deep to put the rod in.

### 5. Work out the running cost

Electricity is billed in units, where 1 unit is 1 kWh. A 1500W rod running for 20 minutes uses about 0.5 units. At ₹7 per unit, that is about ₹3.50 per bucket. Check your own electricity bill for your exact tariff.

| Rod power | Units per 20 minutes | Cost per bucket at ₹7/unit | Cost for 1 bucket a day, 30 days |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1000W | 0.33 | ₹2.33 | ₹70 |
| 1500W | 0.50 | ₹3.50 | ₹105 |

As a rough check on heating time: raising 20 litres of water by 25°C needs about 0.58 units of heat. A 1500W rod supplies 1.5 units an hour, so it takes about 23 minutes with no heat lost. In a cold bathroom expect a little longer. A 1000W rod takes about half as long again.

## Immersion rod safety rules

- Put the rod in the water first, then switch on. Switch off and unplug before you take it out or touch the water.
- Use a plastic bucket, not a metal one.
- Keep the element fully under water but keep the plastic top above the water line.
- Never leave a switched-on rod unattended, and keep children away.
- Use a properly earthed 3 pin socket.
