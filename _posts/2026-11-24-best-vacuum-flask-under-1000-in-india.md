## What "ISI certified" means on a vacuum flask

Since 2024, steel vacuum flasks sold in India have to carry the ISI mark. The Insulated Flask, Bottles and Containers for Domestic Use (Quality Control) Order, 2024 (S.O. 1072(E), dated 5 March 2024) makes BIS certification compulsory for domestic stainless steel vacuum flasks and bottles under IS 17526:2021, with exemptions for export goods and very small Udyam registered micro units. A PIB release from August 2026 repeats that making, storing or selling covered products without the ISI mark is illegal, except for exempted categories.

IS 17526 sets the steel grade (304 or higher for the inner vessel), food grade stoppers and silicone seals, and BPA free polymer closures. It also tests heat retention with water filled at 95°C, plus leakage, staining, impact and strap strength.

### What "24 hours hot" really looks like

Here is the part most listings skip. Flask maker Kohek publishes its own IS 17526 test figures, and they show how much a certified 1 litre flask cools down:

| Flask type (1000 ml) | After 1 hour | After 6 hours | After 12 hours | After 24 hours |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Narrow mouth | 91°C | 80°C | 70°C | 55°C |
| Wide mouth | 88°C | 70°C | 56°C | 42°C |

Source: Kohek BIS information page, water filled at 95°C and kept closed. Actual results drop further if you open the flask often or fill it half way.

So "24 hours hot" really means "warm the next day". Tea poured in at 7 am will still be properly hot at lunch, but by the next morning it will only be warm. The table also shows why mouth size matters: a narrow mouth kept water about 13°C hotter at 24 hours than a wide mouth flask of the same size. BIS's product manual for IS 17526 also shows that licensees test the 1, 6 and 12 hour heat figures every month, but the 24 hour heat test only once in three months and it can be sent out to a lab.

### How to check the ISI mark

Look for the ISI mark with a licence number (CM/L followed by digits) on the bottle or box. You can enter that number in the free BIS Care app to see whose licence it is and what product it covers. If a listing does not mention ISI at all, check the photos or ask the seller before buying.

## How to choose a vacuum flask

### Narrow mouth for hot, wide mouth for cold

If you mostly carry tea, coffee or hot water, pick a narrow or small mouth flask such as the Sumeet. If you mostly carry cold water with ice, a wide mouth like the Cello Flipstyle or Borosil is easier to fill and clean.

### Size

1 litre is right for one adult's day or two people's tea. For a child's school bag, the 500 ml or 750 ml versions of the same listings weigh less. On a long family trip, the Cello Swift also comes in 1.5, 1.8 and 2 litre sizes.

### Care

Fill the flask with hot water for a few minutes before pouring tea in. It keeps the tea hotter for longer. Fill it close to the top, keep it closed and hand wash with a bottle brush. Leave the lid off when you store it so it does not smell. Never microwave a steel flask.

## FAQ

### Which is the best vacuum flask under ₹1,000 in India?

The Milton Flip Lid 1000 at about ₹899 is our pick. It is number 1 in Insulated Bottles on Amazon.in, ISI certified, and has a 1 year warranty. For a lower budget, the Sumeet Thermo1000 at about ₹699 is also ISI certified with a small mouth for better heat retention.

### Does a vacuum flask really keep tea hot for 24 hours?

It keeps it warm, not hot. Kohek's published IS 17526 test figures for a 1 litre narrow mouth flask filled at 95°C show 80°C after 6 hours, 70°C after 12 hours and 55°C after 24 hours. A wide mouth flask of the same size showed 42°C at 24 hours.

### Is ISI mark mandatory for steel flasks in India?

Yes. The Insulated Flask, Bottles and Containers for Domestic Use (Quality Control) Order, 2024 requires domestic stainless steel vacuum flasks to meet IS 17526:2021 and carry the ISI mark, with exemptions only for export goods and very small micro enterprises.

### How do I check if a flask's ISI mark is genuine?

Note the licence number printed next to the ISI mark (it starts with CM/L) and check it in the BIS Care app. The app shows the licence holder and product, so you can see whether it matches the brand on your flask.

### Can I put a steel vacuum flask in the dishwasher or microwave?

Never in a microwave. For the dishwasher, follow the listing: Borosil and Perch say not dishwasher safe, and Milton's listing is contradictory, so hand washing is safest for all of them.
