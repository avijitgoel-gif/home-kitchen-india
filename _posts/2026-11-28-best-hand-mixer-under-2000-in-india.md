## Hand mixer or hand blender? Read the box, not the title

A **hand mixer** has a motor body with two sockets underneath for twin beaters (for cream, egg whites and cake batter) and twin dough hooks. A **hand blender** is a stick with a blade at the bottom, made for soups, purees and smoothies. Our [hand blender guide]({{ "/best-hand-blender-under-2000-in-india/" | relative_url }}) covers the stick type.

Amazon titles mix the two up. The Philips HR3705/10 title says "Hand Mixer, Blender", and Amazon files its item type as "blender" even though it ships only with strip beaters and dough hooks. So check the "Included Components" line on the listing. If it says beaters and dough hooks, it is a hand mixer.

## Is the ISI mark compulsory on hand mixers?

Under the new household appliances order, yes. The Safety of Household, Commercial and Similar Electrical Appliances (Quality Control) Order, 2026 (S.O. 1739(E), dated 6 April 2026) requires household electrical appliances rated up to 250 V single phase to meet IS 302 (Part 1) and carry the ISI mark from 1 October 2026, with extra time for small and micro enterprises.

The specific international safety standard for mixers is IEC 60335-2-14 (kitchen machines). Its scope names "cream whippers", "egg beaters" and "food mixers", and it says hand-held kitchen machines must be Class II or Class III, meaning double insulated or extra low voltage. None of the five listings shows an ISI licence number, so look for "CM/L-" on the box or rating plate and verify it in the BIS Care app.

## Buying guide: how to choose a hand mixer

1. **Wattage:** 250 to 300 W handles cream, egg whites and normal cake batter. Look at 350 W if you often make thick doughs.
2. **Speeds:** 5 speeds are enough. Start on the lowest speed to avoid splashing, as the Philips manual advises, and step up.
3. **Attachments:** you want two beaters and two dough hooks. The iBELL WHISK280 listing names one dough hook, so check the box contents.
4. **Eject button:** all five have one.
5. **Warranty:** Philips gives 2 years outright. iBELL gives 2 years only if you register within 30 days. Lifelong and Inalsa give 1 year.
