## How an egg boiler decides soft, medium or hard

Egg boilers do not have a timer. The water you pour in is the timer. The heater boils the water into steam, the steam cooks the eggs, and the thermostat cuts power once the plate runs dry and heats up. AGARO's listing spells this out: the boiler shuts off "once the specified amount of water has been used", with 35 ml for soft, 55 ml for medium and 120 ml for hard. That is why every boiler ships with a marked measuring cup, and why the "3 modes" on most budget models are really three lines on that cup.

Two things follow from this. First, keep the cup. If you lose it, measure with a kitchen measuring jug or a [kitchen scale]({{ "/best-digital-kitchen-weighing-scale-under-1000-in-india/" | relative_url }}) (1 ml of water weighs about 1 g). Second, expect to adjust. Egg size and how cold the eggs are change the result, so if your hard eggs come out soft, add a little more water next time.

The cup also usually carries a pin. AGARO and Amazon Basics both say piercing the egg with it before boiling helps stop the shell cracking.

## Is the ISI mark compulsory for egg boilers?

From 1 October 2026, yes for most sellers. The Safety of Household, Commercial and Similar Electrical Appliances (Quality Control) Order, 2026 (S.O. 1739(E), dated 6 April 2026) requires household electrical appliances rated up to 250 V single phase to meet IS 302 (Part 1):2024 and carry the ISI mark under a BIS licence. Small enterprises have until 1 January 2027 and micro enterprises until 1 April 2027. The international standard for appliances that heat liquids, IEC 60335-2-15, names egg boilers in its scope, alongside kettles and rice cookers. Look for the ISI mark and a CM/L licence number on the box or rating plate, especially on unbranded ₹299 boilers.

## Egg safety: what soft boiled means for your family

Soft boiled eggs are delicious, but they are not for everyone. The US Food and Drug Administration says fresh eggs, even clean and uncracked ones, may contain Salmonella, and advises cooking eggs until both the yolk and the white are firm. It names children, older adults, pregnant women and people with weak immunity as most at risk. So use the hard mode for those groups.

The FDA also says to eat hard cooked eggs, peeled or in the shell, within 1 week, keep them refrigerated, and not leave cooked eggs out for more than 2 hours. Boiling a week's eggs on Sunday in a 14 egg boiler is fine if they go straight into the fridge.
