An instant geyser is a small 3 to 6 litre water heater with a powerful 3,000 watt element. It sits above a kitchen sink or in a small bathroom and gives you hot water within a couple of minutes of switching on, without heating a big tank you will not use. For a family of two or three that bathes with a bucket, or for anyone who just wants warm water at the kitchen tap in winter, it is the cheapest way to stop boiling water on the stove.

## Instant geyser or storage geyser?

An instant geyser has a small tank and a big element, so it heats fast but runs out fast. Most 3 litre units give you about half a bucket of hot water before the temperature drops, which is what several reviewers describe. That is fine for a kitchen, a quick bucket bath or a single person.

If two or more people bathe back to back, or you use a shower, a 10 or 15 litre storage geyser is the better buy. Storage geysers from 6 to 200 litres must carry a BEE star label, rated on how much heat the full tank loses in 24 hours ([BEE Standards and Labelling](https://beeindia.gov.in/show_content.php?lang=1&level=1&lid=322&ls_id=198), [NZEB guide to electric geysers](https://nzeb.in/knowledge-centre/efficient-appliances/electric-geysers/)). Instant geysers are not covered by the star label, so you will not see a star rating on any of the five picks here. That is normal, not a red flag.

If you only need a bucket of hot water a day and do not want to install anything, an [immersion rod under Rs 1,000]({{ "/best-immersion-rod-under-1000-in-india/" | relative_url }}) is far cheaper.

## How to choose an instant geyser

### 1. Capacity: 3 litre or 5 litre

Pick 3 litres for a kitchen sink or a washbasin. Pick 5 litres or more for a bathroom, especially in north India where winter tap water is very cold.

### 2. Pressure rating

If you live in a high-rise or use a pressure pump, buy a model rated 6 bar or more. All five picks here meet that per their listings.

### 3. Safety features

Look for a thermostat, a separate thermal cut-out, a pressure release valve and anti-siphon or anti-backflow protection. Check for the ISI mark on the product label when it arrives.

### 4. Warranty split

Every brand here gives 5 years on the tank but only 2 years on the product and element. The element and thermostat are the parts most likely to fail, so the 2 year number is the one that matters.

### 5. Budget for installation

None of these geysers is truly plug and play. Brand installation costs about ₹300, and pipes, an angle valve and a 16A plug can add a few hundred rupees more. Add ₹500 to ₹1,000 to the price when you compare.

## Wiring and running cost

A 3000W geyser draws about 13 amps at 230 volts, so it needs a 16A socket and suitable wiring, ideally on its own MCB. Do not run it from a 6A socket, a multi-plug or an extension board.

Running cost is simple arithmetic. A 3000W geyser uses 3 units an hour. Ten minutes of heating uses 0.5 units, which is about ₹3.50 at ₹7 per unit. Twenty minutes a day for a month is about 30 units, or roughly ₹210. ## FAQ

### Which is the best instant geyser under ₹5,000 in India?
For most people, the Havells Instanio 3L at about ₹3,412. It is rated 4.2 from about 14,400 Amazon buyers and has a 3000W element, a 304 stainless steel tank and a 6.5 bar rating. For a bathroom, the V-Guard Zio 5L at about ₹3,999 gives you more hot water.

### Is a 3 litre instant geyser enough for a bathroom?
It is enough for one person taking a quick bucket bath. For two or more people, or in a cold north Indian winter, choose 5 litres or a 10 to 15 litre storage geyser.

### How much electricity does an instant geyser use?
A 3000W geyser uses 3 units an hour. Ten minutes of heating uses 0.5 units, about ₹3.50 at ₹7 per unit.

### Do instant geysers have a BEE star rating?
No. BEE star labelling is mandatory for storage type electric water heaters from 6 to 200 litres. Instant geysers fall outside that scheme, so they do not carry a star label.

### Does an instant geyser come with pipes and a plug?
Usually not. The Havells, V-Guard, Crompton and Racold listings all say or show that the 3 pin plug and connection pipes are bought separately or supplied by the technician at extra cost.
