## First check: will your socket handle it?

This is the check that matters most and is missing from almost every OTG listing. Current is roughly watts divided by voltage, so at 230 V:

- An **800 W** OTG (AGARO 9L, Bajaj 1000TSS) draws about 3.5 A.
- A **1,400 W** OTG (Bajaj 2000TM) draws about 6.1 A.
- A **1,600 W** OTG (iBELL 25L, AGARO 25L) draws about 7 A.

A standard small Indian socket is rated 6 A, which works out to about 1,380 W at 230 V. Plugs come in 6 A and 16 A sizes under IS 1293, and electricians routinely put the microwave and the OTG on a 16 A point. So a 1,400 W or 1,600 W OTG belongs on a 16 A socket, not on a 6 A socket through an adaptor, and not on an extension board. Many boards are only rated 6 A or 10 A in total, which is why our [extension board guide](https://avijitgoel-gif.github.io/gadget-guides-india/best-extension-board-with-usb-under-1000-in-india/) tells you to keep heating appliances off them.

If your counter has only 6 A points, get a 16 A point fitted or buy an 800 W model.

Wattage also sets running cost: 1,600 W for 30 minutes at full power is about 0.8 units (kWh), and 800 W about 0.4 units. The thermostat cycles the elements once the oven is hot, so real use is usually lower.

## Is the ISI mark compulsory on OTGs?

Yes, under the new household appliances order. The Safety of Household, Commercial and Similar Electrical Appliances (Quality Control) Order, 2026 (S.O. 1739(E), dated 6 April 2026) requires electrical appliances for household use rated up to 250 V single phase to meet IS 302 (Part 1):2024 and carry the ISI mark under a BIS licence from 1 October 2026. Small enterprises have until 1 January 2027 and micro enterprises until 1 April 2027, and declared older stock can be sold for up to six months.

The specific safety standard is IS 302 (Part 2/Sec 9), the Indian version of IEC 60335-2-9, which covers grills, toasters and similar portable cooking appliances. The IEC scope lists "portable ovens", roasters and rotisseries by name, so a countertop OTG falls under it. None of the five listings shows an ISI licence number, so look for "CM/L-" on the box or rating plate and check it in the BIS Care app.

### Can I run an OTG on a normal 6A socket?

Only a low power one. A 6 A socket carries about 1,380 W at 230 V. An 800 W OTG is fine, but a 1,400 W or 1,600 W OTG draws about 6 to 7 A and should go on a 16 A socket, without adaptors or extension boards.

### Is the ISI mark compulsory for OTG ovens?

Yes. The household appliances Quality Control Order, 2026 (S.O. 1739(E)) requires the ISI mark from 1 October 2026 for household electrical appliances up to 250 V single phase, with extra time for small and micro enterprises. Portable ovens are covered by the safety standard IS 302 (Part 2/Sec 9).
