## Why steam irons leak, and how to stop it

Leaking water is the most common complaint on every listing in this guide. The Philips, Usha and Bajaj listings all carry the same safety note, which explains most of it:

1. **Let the iron heat up first.** Water only turns to steam when the soleplate is hot. If you press the steam button on a cold iron, the water drips out instead. Philips also says leakage has been observed when the iron is used without heating properly.
2. **Use a cotton or linen setting for steam.** At silk or synthetic settings the plate is too cool to make steam, so turn steam off for those fabrics and dry iron them.
3. **Do not fill the tank to the brim.** Leave a gap at the top.
4. **Store it flat or empty it.** Keep the iron horizontal when not in use, or empty the tank after ironing.
5. **A few drops in a new iron are normal.** The listings say steam irons are tested with water at the factory, so you may find droplets inside a brand new tank.

If leaking continues after all that, it is a defect. Amazon offers a 10 day replacement on these listings for defective items, so test steam on the first day.

## How to choose a steam iron

### 1. Steam rate matters more than wattage

Wattage tells you how quickly the plate heats up. Steam rate, in grams per minute, tells you how much steam reaches the cloth. Everything here sits between 15 and 24 g/min. For daily shirts, 15 to 18 g/min is enough. For bedsheets and heavy cotton, the Tefal's 24 g/min helps.

### 2. Check the warranty, not just the brand

Four of the five irons here come with 2 years of warranty. The Usha has 1 year. With irons, early failure is common in reviews of every brand, so the longer warranty is worth a few hundred rupees.

### 3. Look for the ISI mark

Electric irons are under compulsory BIS certification in India under IS 302 (Part 2/Sec 3), listed in BIS's Scheme I ISI mark products. BIS revised the standard in 2024 and set 23 February 2025 as the last date for the old version. Every iron sold in India should carry the ISI mark and a CM/L licence number, so avoid unbranded listings without one.

## Running cost

An iron's rated wattage is its maximum. The thermostat switches the element on and off once the plate is hot, so real use is lower. At the most, a 1300W iron running for 30 minutes uses 0.65 units of electricity, and the 1800W Tefal uses 0.9 units. As an example, at ₹8 per unit that is about ₹5 and ₹7 for a full half hour session. Your actual tariff depends on your state and slab.

### Why is my new steam iron leaking water?

Usually because steam was used before the iron was hot, at a low fabric setting, or with the tank overfilled. Let it heat fully, use steam only at cotton or linen settings, leave a gap in the tank and store it flat. A few drops in a new tank are normal from factory testing.

### How much electricity does a steam iron use?

At most, a 1300W iron uses 0.65 units in 30 minutes and an 1800W iron uses 0.9 units. Real use is lower because the thermostat cycles the element. At an example rate of ₹8 per unit, that is ₹5 to ₹7 per half hour at the most.

### Do steam irons need an ISI mark in India?

Yes. Electric irons are under compulsory BIS certification to IS 302 (Part 2/Sec 3), so they must carry the ISI mark with a licence number to be sold in India.
