## How to choose a mixer grinder

### 1. 750W is enough for Indian cooking

750W handles idli and dosa batter, dry masalas and chutneys. 500W models struggle with hard grains and long wet grinds. Above 1000W is useful only for very heavy, frequent grinding.

### 2. Count the jars you will actually use

Three jars (large wet, medium dry, small chutney) cover most kitchens. A fourth juicer jar is useful only if you make juice regularly.

### 3. Look for a 2-year warranty and ISI mark

Motor failure is the most common problem. A 2-year warranty and an ISI mark (required for many appliances in India) are good signs of build quality.

### 4. Check service coverage in your city

Philips, Prestige, Bajaj, Butterfly, Crompton and Havells all have wide service networks. Newer online brands may need you to ship the unit for repairs.

### 5. Ignore the MRP

The MRPs in the table range from 1.5 to 2.5 times the selling price. Compare selling prices and rating counts instead.
