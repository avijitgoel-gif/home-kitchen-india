## First, what a "cup" means on these boxes

This is the single most useful thing to know before you compare models. Coffee maker brands count small cups, not the mug on your desk.

- Wonderchef says plainly on the Swift Brew listing that "each cup holds 100 ml", so its 6 cups is 600 ml.
- Morphy Richards lists the Europa at 6 cups and 600 ml, which is also 100 ml a cup.
- Pigeon calls the Brewster a 5 cup machine with a 600 ml jug, so 120 ml a cup.
- AGARO is the honest one. It sells the Royal as "4 big cups" from 600 ml, which is 150 ml a cup.
- An older Philips manual for its drip machines marks large cups at 120 ml and small cups at 80 ml.

If your mug holds 200 ml to 250 ml, a "6 cup" machine fills two or three mugs, and slightly less once the grounds soak up some water. If two of you drink two mugs each in the morning, you want the Philips with its 1.2 litre jug, or plan on two brews.

## Buying guide: what to check before you buy

### Filter coffee decoction or Western style coffee?

Western style drip coffee is lighter and usually drunk black or with a little milk. South Indian decoction is strong, often made from a coffee and chicory blend, and mixed with plenty of hot milk.

Any of these machines will brew both if you change the dose, but the Preethi is built for decoction with its fine cloth filter and steel jar, and its manual is written around it. For black coffee, any of the glass jug models works.

### Reusable filter or paper filter

A reusable mesh or nylon filter saves money and works fine with medium or coarse powder. Paper filters catch more fine particles and are cheap, but they are an ongoing cost. All the models here except possibly the Philips (see above) list a reusable filter in the box. AGARO, Morphy and Philips list the standard #4 filter size, so paper cones of that size will fit if you want them.

### How much coffee to use

The Specialty Coffee Association recommends 55 grams of coffee per litre of water, plus or minus 10 percent, for drip brewing. For a 600 ml machine that works out to about 33 grams. Measuring spoons vary, so weigh it once on a [kitchen scale]({{ "/best-digital-kitchen-weighing-scale-under-1000-in-india/" | relative_url }}) and then note how many spoons that is. The same SCA standard suggests water at about 94°C at the coffee bed and a drip contact time of roughly 4 to 6 minutes. You cannot set temperature on any machine here, so grind size is your main control: coarser if the coffee tastes bitter, finer if it tastes thin.

### Warmer and auto off

A warming plate keeps the jug hot, but coffee left on heat for an hour starts to taste stewed. Philips turns off 30 minutes after brewing, AGARO after 2 hours and Preethi keeps decoction warm for an hour. If you brew and leave for work, pick a model that states an auto off.

### Glass jug or steel

Glass lets you see how much is left and is easy to clean, but can break. The Preethi's steel jar will not break and holds heat. Wonderchef and AGARO use borosilicate glass, which handles heat changes better than ordinary glass.

### Power and plug

These machines draw 450W to 1000W, well within a normal 6A socket. Plug them straight into the wall or a good [extension board](https://avijitgoel-gif.github.io/gadget-guides-india/best-extension-board-with-usb-under-1000-in-india/) rather than a cheap multi plug. Since 1 October 2026, the household electrical appliances Quality Control Order (S.O. 1739(E)) has made the ISI mark mandatory for most mains powered household appliances, with later dates for small and micro enterprises, so look for ISI on the box.

### Can a drip coffee maker make South Indian filter coffee?

Yes. The Preethi Cafe Zest is designed for it, works with coffee and chicory powder, and makes up to 450 ml of decoction in 5 to 8 minutes. Other drip machines can make a stronger brew if you use more powder.

### How many cups does a 6 cup coffee maker make?

About three regular mugs. Brands count a cup as 100 ml to 150 ml. Wonderchef states 100 ml a cup, and a 600 ml jug is called 6 cups by Morphy Richards and Wonderchef and 4 big cups by AGARO.

### Can a drip coffee maker make espresso or cappuccino?

No. Drip machines use gravity, not a pressure pump, so they cannot make espresso or crema, even if the title says espresso. For a milky drink, froth hot milk separately and add the coffee.
