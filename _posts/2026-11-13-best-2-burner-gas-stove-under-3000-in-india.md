## The new BEE star label for LPG stoves

From 1 January 2026, LPG stoves joined India's energy star labelling programme. A Ministry of Power order dated 4 August 2025 (S.O. 3582(E)) sets star levels by the thermal efficiency of the stove's weakest burner:

| Star level | Thermal efficiency |
| --- | --- |
| 1 star | 68% to under 70% |
| 2 star | 70% to under 72% |
| 3 star | 72% to under 74% |
| 4 star | 74% to under 76% |
| 5 star | 76% and above |

These bands are valid until 31 December 2028. The test is clause 26 of IS 4246, the BIS standard for domestic LPG stoves, which on its own only requires each burner to reach 64%. In plain terms, a higher star stove gets more of the gas into your pan, so the same cylinder lasts longer.

None of the five Amazon listings above states a star rating, and older stock made before the order may not have one. If the label matters to you, ask the seller or look for the BEE label on the box when it arrives.

## Why glass tops crack, and how to avoid it

A shattered glass top is the scariest complaint on every glass model here. Pigeon's own manual for the Aster says not to place any hot object on the glass surface and to avoid a tandoor, an inverted vessel or anything that covers the burners. Following that cuts most of the risk:

1. **Never rest a hot pan, lid or ladle on the glass.** Move it to a stand or the burner grid.
2. **Do not use a tandoor or a large vessel that overhangs and traps heat** over the glass.
3. **Keep the flame inside the base of the pan.** A flame that licks up the sides heats the glass around the burner.
4. **Wipe spills after the stove cools,** not with a cold wet cloth on hot glass.

If you cook with very large vessels or a tandoor often, a stainless steel stove like the Longway Stellar avoids the issue.

## How to choose a 2 burner gas stove

### 1. Check the ISI mark

Domestic LPG stoves are under compulsory BIS certification. The Domestic Gas Stoves (Quality Control) Order, 2019, in force since 1 June 2020, requires every LPG stove sold in India to conform to IS 4246 and carry the ISI mark. All five here say they are ISI certified, so skip unbranded listings without a CM/L licence number.

### 2. Glass or steel

Glass tops look better and wipe clean in seconds. Steel tops survive hot vessels and rough use. Choose glass for a modular kitchen if you are careful, steel for heavy daily cooking.

### 3. Manual or auto ignition

Manual stoves have fewer parts to fail. Auto ignition is convenient but the most common failure point in reviews. Either way, you can always light the burner with a lighter.

### 4. Size

The Lifelong models are compact at about 53 cm wide. Measure your counter and your biggest pressure cooker before you buy. If your cylinder runs out often or you want a backup, an [induction cooktop]({{ "/best-induction-cooktop-under-3000-in-india/" | relative_url }}) or a [multipurpose electric kettle]({{ "/best-multipurpose-electric-kettle-under-2000-in-india/" | relative_url }}) makes a good second cooker.

### Is a glass top gas stove safe?

Yes, if you use it as the maker says. Pigeon's manual says never to put hot objects on the glass and to avoid a tandoor or inverted vessel over the burners. Most shattered glass complaints come from heat trapped on the glass. If you cook with large vessels often, choose a stainless steel top.

### Do LPG gas stoves have a star rating now?

Yes. A Ministry of Power order brought LPG stoves into the BEE star label scheme from 1 January 2026. Stars run from 1 star at 68% thermal efficiency to 5 stars at 76% or more, based on the weakest burner.

### Is the ISI mark compulsory for gas stoves?

Yes. Under the Domestic Gas Stoves (Quality Control) Order, 2019, every LPG stove sold in India since 1 June 2020 must meet IS 4246 and carry the ISI mark.

### Can I use these stoves with PNG?

They are sold for LPG. PNG needs a nozzle conversion by an authorised technician. Pigeon says to contact its service partner, and Lifelong says it does not provide the conversion.
